How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions. Tap RV Trip Planner, then tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to stop the next renewal.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app and tap Restore Purchases. You can find it on the subscription screen and in Settings. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
