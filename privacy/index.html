RV Trip Planner Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This policy explains what data the RV Trip Planner app ("the app", "we") uses, why, who processes it for us, and the choices you have. The app has no accounts: we never ask for your name, email address or phone number.

What stays on your phone

Your rig profile, preferences, trips and their routes, saved places, pins, notes and your departure checklist are stored only on your phone. We do not keep a copy on our servers.

What the app sends, and why

  • Route planning. When you plan or change a trip, the app sends the start, stops and destination (as coordinates), your rig's sizes and your route options to our server, which asks TomTom for a route that fits your rig. If you choose "Current location" as the start, the app reads your location once, while the app is open. We do not store these coordinates on our server.
  • Place search. What you type to find a place is sent to our server, which looks up matching places with TomTom. If location is on, a "nearby" search also sends your approximate position at that moment.
  • Places along your route. To show campgrounds, dump stations, propane, fuel and rest areas, the app sends points along your route to our server.
  • Install and subscription identifiers. Each request carries a random identifier created when the app is installed (to limit abuse), and requests that need a subscription carry the anonymous profile id of our subscription provider so our server can check that the subscription is active.

Location

The app uses your location only while it is open ("While Using the App"), to start a route from where you are and to find places near you. It never uses location in the background. You can turn location off in iOS Settings at any time; you can still type a start address.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. Adapty, our subscription provider, receives your purchase and subscription status, an anonymous profile id and Apple Search Ads attribution data, to unlock the app and to measure subscriptions. Adapty also passes subscription events to Google Analytics for Firebase so we can measure subscriptions. You manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used (for example, that the paywall was shown) and Firebase Crashlytics to fix crashes. These Google services receive usage events, device and app information, an app-instance identifier, your approximate region (from your IP address), app performance information and crash reports. They do not receive your trips, routes, searches or notes, and the app does not use your device's advertising identifier for them.

Advertising measurement and tracking (only with your permission)

If the app asks for permission to track (the iOS App Tracking Transparency "Allow Tracking" prompt), and only if you tap Allow, the Meta (Facebook) SDK may receive your device's advertising identifier and app events, and our subscription provider may report purchases to Meta, to measure which of our ads brought you to the app. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", no advertising identifier is shared and every feature works the same. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete my data removes everything the app stored on your phone (trips, routes, places, rig, preferences, checklist, caches) and resets the app's anonymous subscription profile; a new one starts on the next launch. Your subscription itself belongs to your Apple Account: it is not cancelled, and "Restore Purchases" brings it back. Deleting the app also removes its data from your phone. Because we keep no personal data on our servers, there is nothing else to delete; for analytics data you can contact us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
